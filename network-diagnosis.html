StandardInbound Reliability. A ceiling on origin-hub departure variance and mis-sort rate, by destination. G's mis-sort loss is 4 times the next worst market.

The practice: the launch time is fixed. B held its planned launch all 42 days, at the earliest start in the network and the 2nd-highest volume. A fixed launch is a sequence, and a sequence is copyable.
Caveat: B's morning is repeatable; B's outcome isn't. B's trucks arrive on time, but three quarters of the 4.0 points B does lose are middle-mile delay and missort, the same shape as G at a tenth the scale. Scale the sequence, don't hold B up as a model of stability.

The practice: verify at the stow, not at the truck. Catch the error where it happens, not three touches downstream.
Caveat: the rework savings don't generalize. Market C's in-building miss rate is 3.5 times E's, and it spends only one point more of its hours on rework. Justify this standard on on-time delivery; the labor-hour savings will not hold up.
